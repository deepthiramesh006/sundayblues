The story so far

A brand inspired by the in-between.

Sundayblues began with a simple idea: the clothes we reach for most often should be the clothes that make us feel most like ourselves. Inspired by unhurried Sundays, blue skies and the freedom of getting dressed without overthinking it, our concept label celebrates relaxed shapes and wearable colour.

We imagine a wardrobe that moves with you — from campus days and coffee dates to long playlists and spontaneous plans.

What we stand for

Less pressure. More personality.

Our north star is simple: make everyday fashion feel approachable, expressive and easy to wear.

✦

Our mission

To make feel-good everyday style more accessible through versatile pieces and an encouraging, inclusive brand voice.

◎

Our vision

To become a go-to style space for a generation that values comfort, individuality and conscious choices.

♡

Our difference

A blue-led visual identity, relaxed styling and mix-and-match essentials designed to fit real routines.
